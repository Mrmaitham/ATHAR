# Outlier research — 2026-10-01

Method: 22 YouTube searches (football documentary / dark history / crime /
disaster / rise-and-fall / world cup …) → 499 videos → kept long-form (≥ 8 min)
→ for each video's channel, pulled its latest 40 uploads and computed
**ratio = video views ÷ median views of the channel's latest 40 uploads**
(channels with median < 3,000 dropped as noise). "Recency" = the video's
position among the channel's latest 40 uploads (1 = newest); exact upload
dates could not be fetched (YouTube rate-limited the session).
