# LITTLE WONDERS — EPISODE 03 — MISHOO'S BUBBLE PARTY

Episode Theme: Fun Is Bigger When We Share It!
Version: V3 — Production-Ready Storyboard Script (supplied by Maitham, 2026-10-27)
Total Shots: 63 · Shot Duration: 6 s · Story Duration: 6:18
Intro: existing Little Wonders opening song, inserted after Shot 01
Language: English · Audience: 3–12 · Location: front garden, family home, Daiya, Kuwait
Style: high-quality stylized 3D family animation consistent with the approved character references.

## PART 1 — MANDATORY PRODUCTION RULES
1. Character consistency: use only the latest approved reference images; never change faces, hair, clothing, accessories, ages, proportions, colors.
2. ABSOLUTE RULE: MAXIMUM FOUR VISIBLE CHARACTERS PER SHOT. Mishoo counts as one. Characters outside the frame do not count. No extra characters in reflections/windows/backgrounds.
3. Every entrance/exit needs a clear reason and direction; no teleporting; each shot starts from a physically logical continuation of the previous one.
4. Dialogue: natural, short English, age-appropriate. Hoor and Afnan speak like young toddlers. Grandma Khadija warm and calm. Mishoo: cat sounds and body language only.
5. Grandma remains seated in her garden chair, drinks tea, observes, gently helps. Never slapstick, never carries a giant bubble wand. Humor from warm reactions.
6. Six-second shot rule: one clear primary action per shot; dialogue fits naturally; no accelerated movement.
7. Bubble physics: natural; small bubbles float, drift, pop; larger bubbles from ordinary bubble toys, believable; soap residue limited to small amounts; no foam explosions.

## PART 2 — PERMANENT GARDEN LAYOUT
- ZONE A — House entrance: main door and pathway into the garden.
- ZONE B — Grandma's corner: chair, small side table, cup of tea.
- ZONE C — Bubble play area: open grass with a small children's table holding bubble bottles and ordinary wands.
- ZONE D — Mishoo's resting area: soft cushion beside a small tree and low bushes.
- ZONE E — Swing area: children's swing on the opposite side of the garden.
- ZONE F — Garden tap near the house wall.
All six zones belong to the same front garden; door, tree, swing and Grandma's chair keep consistent relative positions.

## ACT 1 — THE SNEAKY BUBBLE (Shots 01–12, 00:00–01:12)
Objective: establish Hoor, Afnan and Mishoo.

**S01 — The Sneaky Bubble** · Zone D · Mishoo (1) · extreme close-up, low angle. Mishoo sleeps; a tiny bubble floats to his nose and lands; whiskers twitch; he sneezes, lifting his head. SFX "Aaa... CHOO!". End: Mishoo stares toward the camera, confused. CUT TO INTRO SONG.
**S02 — Who Did That?** · Zone D · Hoor, Afnan, Mishoo (3) · medium. Girls giggle; Mishoo looks from one to the other. HOOR "Oops! Sorry, Mishoo!" AFNAN "Funny sneeze!" End: Mishoo notices another bubble.
**S03 — The First Chase** · Zone D · Hoor, Afnan, Mishoo (3) · low tracking. Mishoo follows the bubble, reaches with a paw; it floats beyond reach. HOOR "Get it, Mishoo!" End: Mishoo prepares to jump.
**S04 — Too Late!** · Zone D · Mishoo, Hoor, Afnan (3) · side view. Mishoo jumps; bubble drifts up; he lands without catching it. AFNAN "Missed it!" End: Mishoo looks up.
**S05 — Afnan's Turn** · Zone C · Afnan, Hoor (2) · medium close-up. Girls move to the bubble table; Afnan dips her wand and blows too hard; nothing. AFNAN "Huh? No bubble?" End: puzzled with the wand.
**S06 — The Baby Bubble** · Zone C · Hoor, Afnan (2) · close-up. Hoor shows a gentle blow; a tiny bubble appears. AFNAN "Baby bubble!" End: bubble floats toward Zone D.
**S07 — The Tiny Enemy** · Zone D · Mishoo (1) · close-up. Bubble approaches; he leans back; it pops near his nose. SFX POP! End: Mishoo blinks twice.
**S08 — Mishoo's Revenge** · Zone C/D boundary · Hoor, Afnan, Mishoo (3) · medium. Hoor blows; Mishoo taps it; POP; he looks at his paw. HOOR "You got it!" End: paw raised for another.
**S09 — The Wrong Target** · Zone C · Hoor, Afnan, Mishoo (3) · low-angle medium. Afnan blows; Mishoo taps a fallen leaf instead; it sticks to his paw. AFNAN "Mishoo! That's a leaf!" End: he shakes it off.
**S10 — Grandma's Little Smile** · Zone B · Grandma, Hoor, Afnan, Mishoo (4) · medium-wide. Girls approach Grandma's chair, Mishoo follows; Grandma smiles holding tea. GRANDMA "Having fun?" HOOR "So much fun!" End: Hoor raises her bottle.
**S11 — Oh No!** · Zone B · Grandma, Hoor, Afnan, Mishoo (4) · close-up on Hoor. She dips the wand; no solution; turns the bottle upside down; empty. HOOR "Oh no! All gone!" End: Hoor looks toward Grandma.
**S12 — Grandma Has An Idea** · Zone B · Grandma, Hoor, Afnan, Mishoo (4) · medium. Hoor shows the empty bottle; Grandma looks toward the house entrance. GRANDMA "Let's ask Zoozoo!" End: Grandma turns toward the door.

## ACT 2 — ZOOZOO TO THE RESCUE (Shots 13–24, 01:12–02:24)
Objective: introduce Zoozoo naturally; scale up the bubble games.

**S13 — Calling Zoozoo** · Zone B · Grandma, Hoor, Afnan, Mishoo (4) · medium facing the house. Grandma calls toward the open doorway. GRANDMA "Zoozoo! Can you help?" ZOOZOO (O.S.) "Coming!" End: Hoor turns excitedly.
**S14 — The Curious Explorers** · Zone B to D · Afnan, Mishoo (2) · low tracking. Mishoo notices a butterfly near the bushes and walks toward it; Afnan follows. AFNAN "Wait, Mishoo!" End: both enter Zone D. Continuity: Afnan and Mishoo now away from Grandma's chair.
**S15 — Zoozoo Arrives** · Zone A/B · Zoozoo, Grandma, Hoor (3) · medium. Zoozoo exits the house with a bottle of solution and two ordinary wands. ZOOZOO "Bubble rescue!" HOOR "Yay, Zoozoo!" End: Zoozoo walks to the bubble table with Hoor.
**S16 — The Refill** · Zone C · Zoozoo, Hoor (2) · close-up on the table. Zoozoo refills Hoor's bottle; Hoor impatient. HOOR "More! More!" ZOOZOO "Almost ready!" End: Zoozoo closes the bottle.
**S17 — The Bigger Bubble** · Zone C · Zoozoo, Hoor (2) · medium close-up. Zoozoo blows slowly; a larger-than-usual bubble forms. HOOR "Wow! Big bubble!" End: drifts toward the tree.
**S18 — Don't Pop It!** · Zone C/D boundary · Zoozoo, Hoor (2) · side tracking. Hoor reaches; Zoozoo gestures her to stop. ZOOZOO "Wait! Watch it fly!" End: both watch it move toward Zone D.
**S19 — The Return** · Zone D · Afnan, Mishoo, Hoor, Zoozoo (4) · medium-wide. Afnan and Mishoo emerge from the bushes and notice the large bubble. AFNAN "Ooooh! Big one!" End: Mishoo sits beneath the descending bubble.
**S20 — The Bubble Crown** · Zone D · Mishoo, Afnan, Hoor, Zoozoo (4) · close-up on Mishoo. Bubble touches his head fur; POP; small ring of soap remains. HOOR "Mishoo has a crown!" End: Mishoo looks up trying to see his head.
**S21 — The Royal Cat** · Zone D · Mishoo, Afnan, Hoor (3) · medium close-up. Mishoo sits upright, looking important; Afnan bows playfully. AFNAN "King Mishoo!" End: serious meow.
**S22 — The Crown Disappears** · Zone D · Mishoo, Zoozoo, Hoor, Afnan (4) · close-up. Zoozoo wipes the residue with a soft cloth from the bubble table. ZOOZOO "All clean, Your Majesty!" End: Mishoo shakes his ears.
**S23 — The Bubble Lesson** · Zone C · Zoozoo, Hoor, Afnan (3) · medium. Zoozoo demonstrates a slow gentle blow; Afnan imitates; small bubble. ZOOZOO "See? Nice and slow!" End: Hoor prepares to try.
**S24 — The Runaway Bubble** · Zone C toward E · Hoor, Zoozoo, Afnan, Mishoo (4) · tracking following the bubble. Hoor blows a larger bubble; a breeze carries it toward the swing. HOOR "Look! It's going to Noonoo!" End: floats toward Zone E.

## ACT 3 — NOONOO VS. THE BUBBLE (Shots 25–36, 02:24–03:36)
Objective: first major physical-comedy sequence.

**S25 — Zoozoo's Quick Trip** · Zone C/A · Zoozoo, Hoor, Afnan, Mishoo (4) · medium. Zoozoo notices the refill bottle almost empty. ZOOZOO "I'll get more soap!" She walks into the house. Continuity: Zoozoo inside.
**S26 — Follow That Bubble!** · Zone C toward E · Hoor, Afnan, Mishoo (3) · side tracking. Hoor and Afnan walk toward the swing; Mishoo trots behind. AFNAN "Go, bubble, go!" End: bubble approaches the swing.
**S27 — Hello, Bubble!** · Zone E · Noonoo (1) · medium. Noonoo gently swinging notices the bubble; slows the swing with her feet. NOONOO "Whoa! Hello there!" End: watches it drift past.
**S28 — The First Attempt** · Zone E · Noonoo (1) · medium. She steps off the stationary swing and reaches; it floats just beyond her fingertips. NOONOO "Hey! Come back!" End: follows it with her eyes.
**S29 — Left or Right?** · Zone E · Noonoo (1) · front-facing medium. She steps left, bubble drifts right; steps right, it drifts left. NOONOO "Seriously?" End: plants her feet, determined.
**S30 — The Sneaky Bubble** · Zone E · Noonoo (1) · close-up. She reaches with one finger; bubble rises; she stops; it descends again. NOONOO "Aha! Got you!" End: finger hovers beneath it.
**S31 — The Cheering Team** · Zone E · Noonoo, Hoor, Afnan, Mishoo (4) · medium-wide. Hoor, Afnan and Mishoo arrive beside the swing. HOOR "Go, Noonoo!" AFNAN "Catch it!" End: Noonoo smiles confidently.
**S32 — The Perfect Moment** · Zone E · Noonoo, Hoor, Afnan, Mishoo (4) · slow push-in. Bubble floats in front of Noonoo's face; she holds still. NOONOO "Easy... easy..." End: bubble touches her nose tip.
**S33 — POP!** · Zone E · Noonoo, Hoor, Afnan, Mishoo (4) · close-up. Bubble bursts on her nose; small patch of residue above her upper lip. NOONOO "Whaaat?!" End: she opens her eyes.
**S34 — The Bubble Mustache** · Zone E · Noonoo, Hoor, Afnan, Mishoo (4) · medium. HOOR "You have a mustache!" AFNAN "Funny Noonoo!" End: Noonoo touches her upper lip.
**S35 — The Silly Impression** · Zone E · Noonoo, Hoor, Afnan, Mishoo (4) · medium close-up. Noonoo pretends to be a very serious grown-up, arms folded, chin raised. NOONOO "Good morning, everyone!" Hoor and Afnan laugh. End: Noonoo notices Mishoo staring.
**S36 — Mishoo's Verdict** · Zone E · Noonoo, Mishoo, Hoor, Afnan (4) · low-angle two-shot. NOONOO "Do I look funny?" Mishoo tilts his head. MISHOO "Meow!" End: Noonoo laughs.

## ACT 4 — THE GREAT BUBBLE MIX-UP (Shots 37–45, 03:36–04:30)
Objective: build toward the funniest Mishoo moment.

**S37 — One More Round** · Zone C · Hoor, Afnan, Noonoo, Mishoo (4) · medium-wide. Group returns to the bubble table. HOOR "Again! Again!" End: Noonoo picks up another wand.

**S39 — The Dizzy Cat** · Zone C · Mishoo, Hoor, Afnan, Noonoo (4) · close-up. Mishoo follows the two bubbles with quick head movements; sits down puzzled. SFX playful musical sting. End: looks toward the camera.
**S40 — Afnan's Little Mission** · Zone C toward B · Afnan, Hoor, Noonoo, Mishoo (4) · medium. Afnan finds her bottle empty; points to Grandma's table where her spare is kept. AFNAN "Need my bubbles!" She walks toward Zone B. End: Afnan leaves the play area.
**S41 — Zoozoo Returns** · Zone A/C · Zoozoo, Hoor, Noonoo, Mishoo (4) · medium. Zoozoo returns with fresh solution. ZOOZOO "More bubbles, everyone!" NOONOO "Perfect timing!" End: approaches the table.
**S42 — The Bubble Parade** · Zone C · Zoozoo, Hoor, Noonoo, Mishoo (4) · wide. Zoozoo and Hoor blow a small stream; Mishoo follows with curious eyes. NOONOO "Look at him!" End: a bubble floats toward Mishoo's cushion.

**S44 — The Surprise Attack** · Zone D · Mishoo, Hoor, Noonoo, Zoozoo (4) · low close-up. Mishoo reaches a paw above the cushion and taps the bubble; POP; he pulls back startled. NOONOO "He got it!" End: peeks out again.
**S45 — Mishoo's Masterpiece** · Zone D · Mishoo, Hoor, Noonoo, Zoozoo (4) · medium close-up. A second bubble lands on his raised paw; he stares; it pops; he looks at the empty paw. HOOR "Where did it go?" End: he turns his paw over searching.

**S48 — Calling Nadyah** · Zone B · Grandma, Hoor, Noonoo, Mishoo (4) · medium. GRANDMA "Nadyah! Towels, please!" NADYAH (O.S.) "Coming!" End: Hoor notices the wands on the table.
**S49 — Hoor's Next Idea** · Zone B toward C · Hoor, Grandma, Noonoo, Mishoo (4) · medium. HOOR "I'll get our wands!" She walks toward Zone C. End: Hoor exits the Grandma corner.
**S50 — Nadyah Arrives** · Zone A/B · Nadyah, Grandma, Noonoo, Mishoo (4) · medium. Nadyah comes out with clean towels. NADYAH "Towels are here!" End: kneels beside Noonoo.
**S51 — Goodbye, Mustache** · Zone B · Nadyah, Noonoo, Grandma, Mishoo (4) · medium close-up. Nadyah wipes the soap from Noonoo's upper lip. NOONOO "Bye-bye, mustache!" End: Nadyah turns toward Mishoo.
**S52 — Mishoo's Spa** · Zone B · Nadyah, Mishoo, Noonoo, Grandma (4) · close-up. Nadyah wipes the spot from Mishoo's fur with a damp soft cloth; he closes his eyes. NADYAH "There you go, Mishoo!" End: Mishoo relaxes.
**S53 — The Royal Treatment** · Zone B · Mishoo, Nadyah, Noonoo, Grandma (4) · medium. Nadyah finishes drying him; he stretches and meows. NOONOO "He loves his spa!" End: Mishoo walks toward his cushion in Zone D.
**S54 — Everyone Gets Ready** · Zone B/C boundary · Grandma, Nadyah, Noonoo, Mishoo (4) · medium-wide. Noonoo heads to the garden tap to rinse her hands; Nadyah carries the towels toward the house; Mishoo settles on his cushion; Grandma watches smiling. End: Noonoo at Zone F, Nadyah near Zone A, Mishoo in Zone D.

## ACT 6 — MISHOO'S BIG SURPRISE (Shots 55–60, 05:24–06:00)
Objective: Mishoo's strongest comedic payoff; the joy of shared play.

**S55 — The Bubble Team** · Zone C · Hoor, Afnan, Zoozoo (3) · medium. Hoor returns with the small wands; Afnan returns from Grandma's corner with her spare bottle; Zoozoo returns with a bowl of clean water. HOOR "Let's play together!" End: Zoozoo sets the bowl on the table.
**S56 — The Bubble Symphony** · Zone C · Hoor, Afnan, Zoozoo (3) · medium-wide. They take turns blowing: one small bubble, then two, then several. AFNAN "So many!" End: bubbles drift toward Mishoo's area.
**S57 — The Second Team** · Zone C/F boundary · Noonoo, Nadyah (2) · medium. Noonoo returns from the tap; Nadyah returns from putting the towels inside; they pick up two clean wands. NADYAH "Ready, Noonoo?" NOONOO "Let's do it!" End: both blow bubbles toward the garden.
**S58 — Mishoo's Big Moment** · Zone D · Mishoo (1) · low-angle close-up. He rests on his cushion; a small bubble floats to him; he raises a paw and waits patiently; it lands on his paw. End: stares in amazement.
**S59 — The Funniest Pop** · Zone D · Mishoo (1) · extreme close-up, then medium. He brings his paw to his face to inspect the bubble; POP; a tiny startled hop backward onto the cushion; he sits upright pretending nothing happened. SFX POP + playful musical beat. End: looks straight at the camera, very serious.
**S60 — The Bubble Party** · Zone C/D · Hoor, Afnan, Zoozoo, Mishoo (4) · medium-wide. They watch Mishoo's serious face and laugh affectionately; Mishoo meows. HOOR "It's Mishoo's bubble party!" End: Hoor notices Grandma watching from her chair outside the frame.
