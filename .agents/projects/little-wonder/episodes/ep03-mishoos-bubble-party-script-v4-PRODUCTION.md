# LITTLE WONDERS — EPISODE 03 — MISHOO'S BUBBLE PARTY
## V4 — Production Script built on Maitham's rules (2026-10-30)

Based on V3 (Maitham's script). V4 keeps the story and the shot order; it changes the production rules, the camera plan, the transitions, Grandma and Mishoo, and the sound.
Language: English. Audience 3–12. Location: front garden of the family home, Daiya, Kuwait.

---

## PART 1 — THE RULES (Maitham, 2026-10-30)

**R1. No new characters.** Only the approved cast: Hoor, Afnan, Nadyah, Noonoo, Zoozoo, Grandma Khadija, Mishoo. No extra people, children, animals or background figures (reflections and windows included). Props and bubbles are allowed.

**R2. Match the references every time.** Every still and every video is checked against the approved references (shape, size, height, outfit, hair, every detail). If any character differs, the image or video is rejected and redone by me, never shown to Maitham. Approved heights, tallest to shortest: Nadyah, Hoor, Afnan, Mishoo (knee-height). Hoor is taller than Afnan.

**R3. I am the director and the executing director.** I choose the most professional cinematic coverage for each shot (shot size, angle, lens feel, movement). I may add funny or spontaneous moments and sound effects in the background of any shot.

**R4. Colours: cool, natural, never golden.** Neutral daylight, no golden or orange cast, no lit lamps or glow. The colour is calibrated and locked on the very first shot (S01); after Maitham approves S01, its measured values become the numeric target for every later shot (and the minimum brightness and saturation stay 133).

**R5. Chain method.** Shot 1 is made, its last frame becomes the first frame of shot 2, and so on to the last shot, so events and sequence stay linked.

**R6. Maximum four characters in every shot.** When a character leaves a scene it never vanishes: a reason to leave is created first (a call, a task, an object, a chase), then the character walks out of frame in a clear direction.

**R7. Transitions are cinematic, not hard cuts.** See Part 4.

**R8. Grandma Khadija is not always seated.** She moves naturally: stands, walks to the door, reaches for her tea, greets. She is never slapstick. (Kept from earlier instructions: she does not play with the bubbles.)

**R9. Mishoo talks in a few scenes only, and always in a funny way** (squeaky cartoon voice, 5 words or fewer, the kids react naturally). The children always speak more than he does. In his other shots he meows and acts.

**Naming:** "Grandma" (never "Tita"). The name Afnan is spelled "Afnann" in dialogue notes and pronounced AF-nahn. Zoozoo = ZOO-zoo. Mishoo = MEE-shoo.

## PART 3 — WORKING METHOD (how every shot is made)

1. **Stills first.** For each shot I make a start frame and an end frame, check them against the references (R2), and show them to Maitham for approval before video. In a chained shot the start frame is the previous shot's approved end frame (the same file).
2. **Video** is generated between the two approved frames, so the camera, the sizes and the ending are controlled.
3. **Dialogue and sound:** the dialogue is generated as separate audio with correct name pronunciation, then mixed with sound effects and a music bed. (The voice approach is tested on one shot first; if mouth sync is not good enough we go back to the model's own voice.)
4. **Automatic check per shot** (before I show anything): background stability, character size against the references, brightness and saturation, colour against the locked target, join with the previous shot, transcript of the dialogue. A failed shot is redone by me.
5. **Pilot first:** the first chapter (S01–S08) is made completely, and its first-pass success rate is reported before we go on.
6. **Delivery:** I send the merged video for the requested range with a short honest error report.

**Camera language.** Each shot has a declared shot size. Allowed in one shot: a locked frame, or ONE simple declared move (slow push-in, slow side track), where the end still shows the new framing. No zoom or drift that is not declared. Characters never walk toward the camera; they cross the frame sideways, or enter and exit at the frame edges.

## PART 4 — TRANSITIONS (cinematic)

- **[CONT] Continuous chain** inside the same zone: the end frame is the next start frame, no visible edit.
- **[MATCH] Match-on-action** between zones: a character exits frame right (with a reason); a 10–12 frame dissolve overlaps with the next zone where the same character enters frame left.
- **[BUBBLE] Bubble match** (the episode's signature): a bubble ends large and centred in the last frame, and the next shot opens focused on a bubble in the new zone. Used for the big scene changes.
- **[WIPE] Foreground pass:** a character or Hoor's raised hand passes close to the lens and fills the frame; the next shot opens from that same blur.
- **[INTRO]** after S01 the existing Little Wonders opening song, entered with a bubble-pop dissolve.
